Merge Sort

Splits the array into halves, sorts each half recursively, then merges the two sorted halves into one sorted array.

  • Best / Avg / Worst: O(n log n)
  • Space: O(n)
  • Stable: Yes

Quick Sort

Picks a pivot, moves smaller items to its left and bigger items to its right, then repeats on both sides.

  • Best / Avg: O(n log n)
  • Worst: O(n²)
  • Space: O(log n)
